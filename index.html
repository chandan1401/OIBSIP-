A tiny roastery with a big appetite for flavour

Lumen Brew began with one secondhand roaster and a stubborn idea: great coffee should not need a café queue. Today a small team roasts every batch by hand, tasting each one before it ships.

We pay growers above fair-trade prices and keep our packaging fully recyclable, because good coffee should leave a good footprint.
